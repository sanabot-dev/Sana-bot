# Privacy Policy — Sana

[English](PRIVACY.md) • [Português](PRIVACY_PT-BR.md) • [Español](PRIVACY_ES.md)

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Sana may process, why it is used, how it may be stored, and the choices available to users.

By using Sana, you acknowledge that information necessary to provide the bot's features may be processed as described in this Policy.

---

## 1. About this Privacy Policy

Sana is a multilingual K-pop photocard collecting bot for Discord.

Sana provides features including card collection, Drops, GENs, boxes, virtual currency, Market transactions, trades, transfers, Wishlists, NFT protection, Blacklists, missions, rankings, events, reminders, rewards, and other collection-related systems.

This Privacy Policy applies to Sana's operation through Discord and to Sana-related services, integrations, support processes, and infrastructure where applicable.

---

## 2. Information We Process

Sana processes only information reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the service.

Depending on the features you use, this may include:

- Discord user IDs;
- Discord server, channel, thread, message, or interaction identifiers where required;
- your selected or detected language preference;
- usernames or display names when required by specific features;
- profile information voluntarily added through Sana, such as a Sana profile bio;
- card inventory and ownership information;
- card acquisition and ownership history;
- Candy Coins, Tickets, virtual bank balances, and other economy information;
- boxes, GENs, event items, and other virtual items;
- Market listings, purchases, sales, prices, and transaction history;
- trades, transfers, participants, offered cards, and transaction status;
- Wishlist, NFT, Blacklist, ping, and collection preferences;
- cooldowns, reminders, missions, rewards, and progression;
- giveaway participation and results;
- collection achievements and rankings;
- supporter, subscription, or Booster status used to provide applicable benefits;
- information associated with Sana-specific features, such as the `/sana` status system;
- moderation, audit, fraud-prevention, and technical integrity records;
- technical information required to diagnose errors and maintain the service.

Not every user will have every category of information associated with their account.

---

## 3. Discord Information

Sana primarily identifies users through Discord IDs.

Depending on the feature being used, Sana may also process information made available through Discord, such as:

- username or display name;
- profile image;
- server membership;
- server roles;
- language/locale;
- presence or custom status;
- channel, thread, or message identifiers;
- interaction information.

Some of this information may only be processed temporarily and is not necessarily stored in Sana's database.

Sana does not request or store your Discord password or Discord authentication token.

---

## 4. Collection, Economy, and Activity Data

To maintain persistent progress, Sana stores information related to your use of the collection and economy systems.

This may include:

- owned cards and their unique identifiers;
- previous card owners and ownership timestamps;
- how a card was obtained;
- packs, boxes, and GEN items;
- Candy Coins and Tickets;
- virtual bank information and rewards;
- Market listings and completed Market sales;
- trade and transfer history;
- Combine or similar card-processing history;
- missions and mission progress;
- cooldown usage;
- event rewards and gift claims;
- rankings and collection achievements.

Some historical records may remain necessary even after a particular card or item changes ownership so Sana can maintain transaction integrity, rankings, abuse prevention, and technical auditing.

---

## 5. Preferences and Collection Settings

Sana may store preferences you configure, including:

- Wishlist rules;
- NFT protection rules;
- Blacklist rules;
- Market alerts;
- ping preferences;
- reminder settings;
- language preference;
- selected profile settings;
- collection or ranking preferences.

These settings are associated primarily with your Discord user ID.

---

## 6. Messages, DMs, and Interactions

Sana may process messages, attachments, interactions, buttons, forms, commands, or other content when required for a feature to function.

For example, some authorized upload workflows may read image attachments sent through Discord.

Ordinary message content is not intentionally stored as a general message history, user profile, or conversational archive by Sana.

Some interaction information may remain temporarily in memory while the bot is running to allow a command, reminder, DM, or interaction to complete. Temporary interaction credentials are not intentionally persisted as part of Sana's user database.

Information explicitly submitted through a Sana feature may be stored when necessary for that feature. For example, profile text voluntarily entered into a Sana profile may remain stored until changed or removed.

---

## 7. Presence and Status Information

Certain Sana features may use Discord presence or custom status information.

When a user participates in a feature that depends on a specific Sana-related status, Sana may process and store:

- the user's Discord ID;
- relevant server ID;
- whether the qualifying status is active;
- the applicable status text;
- timestamps related to status changes.

Sana does not use presence information to create advertising profiles or sell behavioral information.

---

## 8. Supporters, Subscriptions, and Payments

Sana may provide optional supporter or subscription-related benefits.

Payments may be handled through external payment platforms or payment processors. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Sana does not intentionally store:

- full payment card numbers;
- card security codes;
- online banking passwords;
- payment account passwords.

Sana may receive and retain limited information necessary to verify a payment or provide a benefit, such as:

- a transaction or event identifier;
- Discord user ID;
- subscription or supporter tier;
- payment status;
- whether a qualifying payment was successfully processed;
- the Sana reward associated with that payment.

Where a user voluntarily provides payment-related information through a support ticket, direct message, or other support channel, limited information may be visible to authorized Sana staff for the purpose of confirming or resolving the transaction.

Users should never send full card credentials, passwords, security codes, or other unnecessary financial credentials to Sana staff.

## 9. Optional Integrations and Third-Party Services

Sana relies on third-party services and infrastructure to operate.

These services may include categories such as:

- Discord platform services;
- hosting and server infrastructure;
- cloud storage and content delivery;
- payment and subscription processing;
- bot listing and voting platforms;
- analytics or operational services;
- optional external integrations selected by the user.

Sana does not sell personal data to these services or to advertisers.

Information may be transmitted to or processed by third parties only when reasonably necessary to provide Sana, maintain infrastructure, complete a feature, verify a payment, provide an integration, comply with legal obligations, or protect the service.

Optional integrations are used when the user chooses to connect or use the applicable feature.

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Users are encouraged to review those policies before using an external integration.

## 10. How We Use Information

Information processed by Sana may be used to:

- provide bot commands and features;
- maintain inventories and collections;
- manage the virtual economy;
- process Market transactions, trades, and transfers;
- maintain cooldowns and reminders;
- provide events, missions, rewards, and rankings;
- apply Wishlist, NFT, Blacklist, and alert preferences;
- provide supporter or subscription benefits;
- prevent duplication, fraud, exploits, and abuse;
- investigate technical problems;
- provide user support;
- maintain service security and integrity;
- improve reliability, performance, and usability;
- comply with applicable legal requirements.

Sana does not sell user information.

Sana does not use personal data for targeted advertising.

## 12. Technical Logs and Security Records

Sana may generate technical logs required to operate and troubleshoot the service.

Logs may include information such as:

- timestamps;
- command or system context;
- Discord identifiers;
- server or channel identifiers;
- error names and stack traces;
- database or network errors;
- service health information;
- security or abuse-related events.

Technical logs are retained only for a limited period as reasonably necessary for troubleshooting, security, abuse prevention, and service maintenance.

Retention may vary depending on the type of record and the infrastructure used.

## 13. Data Storage and Security

Sana's persistent data may be stored using databases, hosting infrastructure, cloud services, and backups used to operate the bot.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to reduce unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration of information.

These measures may include:

- restricted server access;
- separation of credentials from source code;
- access controls;
- private administrative systems;
- database integrity protections;
- backups;
- infrastructure security features.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 14. Data Retention

Data is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to:

- operate Sana;
- preserve user progress;
- maintain the virtual economy;
- provide requested features;
- preserve transaction integrity;
- resolve disputes;
- prevent fraud, abuse, or exploits;
- investigate technical problems;
- meet applicable legal or operational requirements.

Different categories of information may therefore be retained for different periods.

Sana does not promise a single fixed retention period for every category of data.

## 15. Backups

Sana's infrastructure may maintain backups for reliability, recovery, or disaster protection.

When information is deleted from the active service, copies may temporarily remain in backups until those backups expire, rotate, or are safely overwritten through normal backup procedures.

Backup copies are not intended to be used to restore individually deleted user information except where necessary for security, legal compliance, or recovery from a significant technical incident.

## 16. Data Access, Correction, and Deletion Requests

Users may request information about their Sana data, request correction where appropriate, or request deletion of personal data by contacting the Sana team through the official support server.

Requests may require reasonable verification that the requester controls the relevant Discord account.

Deleting Sana data may permanently remove or affect:

- cards and collection progress;
- Candy Coins and Tickets;
- virtual bank balances;
- boxes, GENs, and other items;
- Market data;
- Wishlist, NFT, and Blacklist preferences;
- missions and achievements;
- rankings;
- reminders;
- supporter-related Sana records;
- other account progress.

Certain limited records may be retained when reasonably necessary for:

- fraud and abuse prevention;
- security;
- transaction integrity;
- dispute resolution;
- enforcement of restrictions;
- legal obligations.

Where deletion from active systems is possible, residual copies may remain temporarily in backups as described above.
