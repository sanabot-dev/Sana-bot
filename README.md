# 🍭 Sana

A multilingual K-pop photocard collecting bot for Discord.

Collect your favorite idols, trade with other collectors, participate in events, and build your collection through Drops, GENs, boxes, and the global Market.

[Invite Sana](https://sanabot.win/invite-bot) • [Support Server](https://discord.gg/sana-bot)

---

## ✨ Features

🎴 Photocard collecting  
🍭 1LP, 2LP and 3LP cards  
🔄 Trading and transfers  
🏷️ Global marketplace  
💗 Wishlist and NFT protection  
🚫 Custom blacklist  
🎁 Boxes and limited-time events  
🍬 Candy Coins and Tickets  
✨ GEN system  
🎯 Missions and rewards  
🏆 Collection rankings  
🌎 English • Português • Español

---

## 🎴 Collect

Use Drops, Daily rewards, GENs, boxes, and events to expand your collection.

Cards can belong to different idols, groups, eras, and events, allowing collectors to build anything from a favorite idol collection to complete group catalogs.

## 🔄 Trade

Trade cards directly with other collectors through Sana's interactive trade system.

## 🏷️ Market

List cards globally, find cards from your Wishlist, and buy directly from other collectors.

## 💗 Organize your collection

Wishlist, NFT protection, Blacklist, inventory filters, and collection tools help you customize exactly how you want to collect.
